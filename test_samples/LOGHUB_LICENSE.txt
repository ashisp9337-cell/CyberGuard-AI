loghub_openssh_real_attacks.log and loghub_zookeeper_normal.log are the 2,000-line samples of the OpenSSH and
ZooKeeper logs from LogHub (https://github.com/logpai/loghub, commit dd61d0952749ee7963bde24220d1be5ede023033),
used unchanged as CyberGuard demo inputs. The OpenSSH log is from a real lab server that was under real password
attacks from the internet.
