CyberGuard AI - Safe Browsing

This extension asks your own CyberGuard AI on this computer about each website before it opens, and warns you about dangerous ones. Nothing is sent to any other server (unless you switch on "threat lists" below). Protection stays OFF until you switch it on.

1. Connect to your CyberGuard

In CyberGuard go to Settings → Browser extension, press Connect a browser and paste the key here.

2. Your permission

3. Websites you trust

One website name per line (for example intranet.mycollege.ac.in). These are never checked.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Firefox. Open source: the extension folder of your CyberGuard project.
